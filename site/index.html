The Solution

How HomeGuarantee works

A private-market platform that converts landlord fear into a guaranteed financial position.

1

Landlord enrolls a unit

A private landlord lists their rental unit and receives a HomeGuarantee — covering up to $20K in property damage, unpaid rent (3 months), and vacancy loss. All paperwork handled by HomeGuarantee.

2

Case manager matches a tenant

A case manager from The Way Home's network finds a compatible unit on the platform — searchable by size, zip, and FMR compatibility. Tenant profiles pre-verified through HMIS.

3

Tenancy begins with rent guaranteed

Rent deposits on the 1st — before housing authority transfers settle. A tenancy health system triggers proactive case manager contact before problems escalate.

4

Claims paid within 30 days

When landlords file a claim, we pay within 30 days via ACH — no bureaucratic delays. The speed of payment is a marketing event: it proves the guarantee is real.
