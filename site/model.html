💼 Business Model · Phase 1→5

Four revenue tiers. One mission-aligned structure.

HomeGuarantee monetizes from both sides of the housing market: guarantee platform fees from demand-side stakeholders (HCV landlords, managed-service landlords, housing authorities) and renovation exchange fees from supply-side owners (Stabilization tier + Works renovation contracts). Grant income bridges the gap to break-even.

The Works Program

How formerly homeless employees renovate housing for themselves

1

Owner commits

Owner agrees to 18-month below-FMR lease ($200–350/mo) in exchange for renovation. HCAD targeting identifies distressed/tax-delinquent properties first.

2

Works crew scopes

Works employees (currently or formerly homeless) complete a habitability assessment. Scope: mold, electrical safety, plumbing, HVAC basics, weatherproofing. No cosmetic work.

3

Renovation exchange

Works crew completes the renovation. Non-delinquent owners pay $175 contract fee. Delinquent owners get renovation free in exchange for lease commitment only.

4

Tenant moves in

Stabilization-eligible tenants (SSI recipients at 30% income share = ~$290/mo) placed by The Way Home. Works employees must NOT co-reside in Stabilization units due to SSI offset risk.

⚠️
SSI-Works Structural Constraint: Works employment reduces SSI benefits. Tenants in Stabilization units are SSI-dependent (30% of ~$967/mo = ~$290/mo rent). Works employees must not be placed as tenants in the same units they renovated. Co-location creates an SSI offset loop that undermines tenancy. Kill Switch K9 validates this before Month 22.

HCV Guarantee Fund rules

FLOOR
$500K minimum reserve. If breached, no new units enrolled until fund recovers. Covers catastrophic claim scenario (1 large unit, repeated claims).
CLAIMS
12% claim rate × $3,500 avg payout = $420/unit/year expected loss. 15% churn ensures gradual portfolio refresh, reducing correlated risk.
WAIVER
3-month vacancy waiver per unit. Not paid from reserve — it's a revenue concession (zero fees during vacant months), not a cash outlay.

Stabilization reserve rules

PILOT
$100K reserve funds 10-unit pilot starting Month 22. Held separately from HCV fund — no commingling.
TRIGGER
>20% default rate at the 6-month review is an automatic pause trigger. No new Stabilization units enrolled until root-cause analysis complete.
CLAIMS
18% claim rate × $2,800 avg = $504/unit/year. Higher claim rate reflects no HA backstop, lower-value units, and distressed owner base.
