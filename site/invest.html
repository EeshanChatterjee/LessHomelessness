Why Not a Nonprofit?

The case for a private-market structure

The nonprofit ceiling

Open Doors Chicago raised $4.5M as a nonprofit and housed 1,200 people. After 6 years, they still depend 100% on annual grant cycles. Any year the grant doesn't renew, 1,200 households are at risk. They cannot scale beyond the grant capacity. The model doesn't compound.

The company advantage

HomeGuarantee generates platform fees that self-fund operations by Year 3. The guarantee fund accrues from operations. New cities are funded by Houston's EBITDA — not by another grant. Grants accelerate us; we don't depend on them. This is why we're a company, not a program.
